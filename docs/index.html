Glossary

Strategy
The setup tested (family) and the variant of its rules. Hover over a name for the full rules.
Universe
The stocks the strategy could trade: today's NIFTY 500 or NIFTY 50 members.
Exit
How winners are closed. 'Target X%' sells when price rises X% above entry. 'No target' holds until the trend exit (a close below EMA61 on the signal timeframe) or the stop. Every trade also has a stop loss.
Win %
Share of closed trades that made money after fees and slippage. A high win rate alone means little: read it together with Avg win %, Avg loss % and Payoff.
Avg trade %
Average net return per closed trade, on the money put into that trade.
Avg trade ₹
Average net profit or loss per closed trade in rupees. Unlike the %, it reflects position size.
Avg win %
Average net return of the winning trades only.
Avg loss %
Average net return of the losing trades only. Usually close to the stop distance plus costs.
Payoff
Average win divided by average loss. A payoff of 3 means the typical winner is three times the typical loser. Low win rates need high payoffs to make money.
Expectancy R
Average net profit per rupee risked. 1R is the planned loss if the stop is hit (about ₹1,000 here). +0.5R means each trade earns half of what it risks on average; below 0 the strategy loses money.
CAGR %
Compound annual growth rate: the steady yearly return that would turn ₹1 lakh into the final equity.
Max DD %
Maximum drawdown: the largest fall from an equity peak to a later low. The pain you must sit through.
CAGR / DD
CAGR divided by the size of the max drawdown. Return per unit of pain; higher is better. Useful for ranking strategies with different risk.
Trades
Number of closed trades. Fewer than about 100 means the result rests on a small sample.
Trades / yr
Closed trades per year: how busy the strategy keeps you, and how often you pay costs.
Max losing streak
Most losing trades in a row (ordered by exit date). Expect a streak at least this long again.
Median days
Median holding period in calendar days. Under 365 days, gains are taxed as short-term (20%); over 365 days as long-term (12.5%). Tax is not deducted in these results.
Deployed %
Average share of the account held in open positions. The rest sits idle as cash.
Skipped
Signals not taken because all 8 slots were full or cash ran out. Many skips mean the result depends on which signals got the slots (here: tightest stop first).
Costs % of gross
Fees and slippage as a share of profit before costs. Above 100% (or '>100') means the strategy would make money before costs but loses after them.
Report
Link to the full PDF report: rules, equity and drawdown charts, and all results for that strategy.
